Delete your OGSTYLE account

This page explains how to delete your account in the OGSTYLE app (by Dennis Freelance Service) and what happens to your data.

In the app

  1. Open OGSTYLE and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and tap Delete account at the bottom.
  3. Read what happens, choose a reason if you like, and type DELETE to confirm.

You are signed out on every device straight away. Changed your mind? Sign in again within 30 days and your account stays.

Without the app

Email support@ogstyle.app with the subject “Delete my account” and the mobile number you sign in with. We may ask you to confirm by a code sent to that number.

What is deleted

After 30 days: your name, email address, profile photo and booking preference, your sign-in sessions and your device's notification token. The account is closed and can no longer be used to sign in.

What is kept, and for how long

Your past bookings and payment records are kept as financial records for the period described in our Privacy Policy, then deleted.
