Refund & Cancellation Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective 10 November 2026

In short

  • Cancel before your booking's cancellation deadline and you get your whole advance back.
  • After the deadline you can't cancel, and the advance is not refunded, whether or not you come.
  • If the shop or barber cancels, you get your whole advance back, whenever they cancel.
  • We start a refund within 3 working days. Your bank usually credits it 5–7 working days after that.

1. What you pay, and to whom

When you book through the OGSTYLE app, you pay a booking advance online. The advance is OGSTYLE's booking fee. Each shop's advance is a percentage of the booking total, with a minimum and a maximum. You see the exact amount before you pay.

You pay the rest of the price directly to the shop when you visit, in cash or UPI. OGSTYLE does not collect that money, so this policy covers only the advance. Any refund of money you paid at the shop is between you and the shop.

Some shops may not ask for an advance. Nothing is paid online for those bookings, so there is nothing for OGSTYLE to refund.

Payments are processed by Razorpay. OGSTYLE never sees your card, UPI or bank details.

2. Your cancellation deadline

Each shop sets how long before an appointment free cancellation closes. If the shop hasn't chosen, it is 2 hours before the appointment starts.

The app shows the deadline as a clock time, for example “Cancel before 4:30 PM for a full refund”. You see it on the booking screen before you pay, and in the booking's details afterwards.

The deadline shown when you book is the one that applies to that booking, even if the shop later changes its setting.

3. When the advance is refunded

What happens Your advance
You cancel before the deadline Refunded in full
The deadline has passed. The app no longer lets you cancel or change the booking. Not refunded
You don't arrive for your appointment. The shop may mark it as a no-show. Not refunded
The shop or barber cancels your booking, at any time Refunded in full
Your payment went through, but only after your slot's hold ran out, so the booking could not be confirmed Refunded in full. You don't need to ask.
You were charged twice, or money left your account but no booking was confirmed Refunded in full. Write to us with the details if you haven't heard from us.

A full refund is the whole advance you paid. We do not deduct payment-gateway charges.

About the hold: when you start paying, your time slot is held for you for 10 minutes so nobody else can take it. If the payment doesn't complete in that time, the hold ends and the slot is freed. Nothing is charged unless your payment completes.

Why the advance isn't refunded after the deadline: a late cancellation or a no-show leaves the barber with an empty chair that someone else could have booked. The advance is what makes a booking a real commitment. That is also why it is kept small.

4. Changing your booking

Before the deadline, you can move your booking to another available time or barber at the same shop, at no charge. Your deadline then moves with the new appointment time. After the deadline, the booking can no longer be changed.

5. Coupons

If a booking is cancelled, by you before the deadline or by the shop, any coupon you used on it is released. You can use it again while it is still valid. A coupon used on a booking that ends as a no-show counts as used.

6. How and when refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the same payment method you paid with, through Razorpay. We cannot refund to a different account, in cash, or as app credit.

We start your refund within 3 working days of the cancellation. Once it has started, it usually reaches you in 5–7 working days, depending on your bank. Your booking in the app shows the refund status.

If the refund hasn't reached you within 12 working days of the cancellation, write to us with your booking code (shown on the booking in the app). We will trace it with Razorpay and tell you where it is.

To cancel a booking, open the Bookings tab, choose the booking and tap Cancel. If the app won't let you cancel and you think it should, contact us before your appointment time.

7. The service itself

Haircuts, grooming and beauty services are provided by the shop, not by OGSTYLE. If you are unhappy with a service, please raise it with the shop first. Questions about the price you paid at the shop are for the shop to settle.

Tell us too. OGSTYLE may, at its discretion, refund your advance where we think that is fair. We don't promise to in every case, and we don't rule it out.

8. Questions and complaints

Write to support@ogstyle.app or see Contact Us for our phone number, address and Grievance Officer. We acknowledge complaints within 48 hours and aim to resolve them within one month.

The policy in force when you made a booking applies to that booking. If we change this policy, we will update the version and effective date at the top of this page.
