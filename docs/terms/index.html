1. What OGSTYLE is

OGSTYLE is an app for booking appointments at independent barbershops and salons. We run the app and take bookings. The shops provide the services. Each shop is an independent business. It sets its own services, prices, hours and cancellation deadline, and is responsible for the work it does.

Delivery: OGSTYLE sells no physical goods, and nothing is shipped. What you book is an appointment, delivered in person at the shop at the time you booked. You get your booking confirmation in the app as soon as your payment is confirmed.

2. Your account

  • You must be 18 or older to create an account. A parent or guardian can book for a child from their own account.
  • You sign in with your mobile number and a one-time code. Keep your phone secure. Anyone who can receive your codes can use your account.
  • Give your real name and a number you use, so the shop can reach you.
  • One person, one account.

3. Bookings and payment

  • When you book, the app shows the total price, the booking advance you pay online now, and the amount you pay at the shop. The advance is OGSTYLE's booking fee. You pay the rest directly to the shop.
  • A booking is confirmed only when our payment provider, Razorpay, confirms your payment to us. The app tells you when it is confirmed. If your payment doesn't complete while your slot is held, the booking is not made.
  • Prices are set by the shop and shown in Indian rupees. The price you are shown when you pay is the price for that booking. A later change by the shop does not affect it.
  • Please arrive on time. A shop may not be able to serve you if you are late, and the booking may be treated as a no-show.

4. Cancellations and refunds

Cancellations, rescheduling and refunds of the advance follow our Refund & Cancellation Policy. In short: cancel before your booking's deadline for a full refund of the advance. After the deadline, the advance is not refunded. If the shop cancels, you are always refunded in full.
