1. What we collect, and why

If you book with OGSTYLE

  • Your mobile number. This is how you sign in. We send a one-time code to it. The code is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form and expires within minutes.
  • Your name, so the shop knows who is coming.
  • Optional: your email address, and whether you usually book men's or women's services (used to show you relevant services first). You can leave these blank or clear them in your profile.
  • Your bookings: the shop, barber, services, time, price, any coupon, cancellations and their reason, and whether the advance was paid or refunded.
  • Payment records: the amount, the status, and Razorpay's reference numbers for each payment and refund. Card, UPI and bank details go to Razorpay and never reach us.
  • Your device's notification token, with the app version and your chosen language, so we can send booking notifications. We send only notifications about your own bookings and refunds (confirmations, reminders, cancellations by the shop, refunds). We send no advertising notifications. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings.
  • Your in-app notifications, so you can read them later in the app.
  • Sign-in sessions, which keep you signed in on your device.

If you work at a shop (owner or staff)

  • Your name, mobile number, your role at the shop, and the services and hours you work.
  • The shop's location, if you capture it with your phone's GPS, with its accuracy and who captured it. Customers see the shop's location so they can find it.
  • A record of changes made to the shop (for example, prices, hours or cancellations), with who made them and when.

From everyone who uses the app

  • Server logs: when the app talks to our servers, we record the time, the page of the app requested, the result, your IP address and your device's browser/app identifier. We use these to keep the service running and to investigate faults and abuse.
  • Crash reports: if the app or our server hits an error, a report of the error is sent to Sentry. We remove phone numbers, codes, tokens and locations from these reports before they are sent.

We use this data to provide bookings and sign-in, take and refund payments, send booking notifications, give support, keep the service secure, and meet legal and tax obligations. We don't use it for advertising, and we don't sell it.

2. Your location

If you allow it, the app reads your phone's location to show the nearest shops first. That position is sent to our server only to sort the list. It is not saved: not in your account, not in our server logs, not in crash reports. If you don't allow location, the app still works and shows shops sorted another way.

Shop owners may also use the phone's location to set where their shop is (see above). That is the shop's location, not a customer's.

3. Who we share it with

  • The shop you book with sees your name, mobile number and booking, so they can serve you and contact you about it.
  • Razorpay processes payments and refunds. To save you typing, the checkout is pre-filled with your name, phone number and email (if you gave one). You can change them there.
  • Expo and Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) deliver push notifications to your phone. The notification text includes the shop's and barber's names. It never includes amounts.
  • Our hosting providers store and run the service: Render (application servers), Supabase (database, Mumbai region) and GitHub Pages (this website).
  • Sign-in codes will be sent by SMS through a provider we will name here before SMS sign-in starts.
  • Sentry receives crash reports, cleaned as described above.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, for example a valid order from a court or the police, or tax authorities.

Some of these providers may store data outside India.

4. How long we keep it

  • Your profile, for as long as your account is open.
  • Bookings and payment records, for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires us to keep them, even after you close your account. They are financial records.
  • Server logs, for 7 days.
  • Sign-in codes expire within minutes.
  • Notification tokens stop being used as soon as your device or the delivery service tells us they are no longer valid.

5. Deleting your account

To delete your account, email support@ogstyle.app from any address, with the subject “Delete my account” and the mobile number you sign in with. We may ask you to confirm the request from the app or by a code sent to that number, so nobody else can delete your account.

Within 7 working days we will close your account and sign you out on every device. You will no longer be able to sign in with it.

Your past bookings and payment records are kept as financial records for the period in section 4, then deleted. Bookings you have already paid for and not cancelled stay with the shop. Cancel them first if you don't want to go.

6. Your rights

Under Indian data protection law you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct or complete it (you can also edit your name and email in the app);
  • erase it, as described above;
  • withdraw a consent you gave (for example, location or notifications);
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights if you die or cannot act;
  • resolve a complaint about how we handle your data.

Write to our Grievance Officer at support@ogstyle.app (details on Contact Us). If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Sign-in codes are stored only in hashed form, and sessions can be revoked. Access to shop data is checked on every request. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

8. Children

You must be 18 or older to create an OGSTYLE account. A parent or guardian can book for a child from their own account.

9. This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts. Like any website, its host (GitHub Pages) records visitors' IP addresses in its server logs.

10. Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we will update the version and effective date above. For a change that matters, we will also tell you in the app. Questions: support@ogstyle.app.
