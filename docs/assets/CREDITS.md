# Image credits

## App screenshots (`screens/`)

Captured 2026-11-21 from the real OGSTYLE app (Android emulator, debug build)
against a **scratch database seeded with demo data only**
(trim-time `SEED_PROFILE=screens`). No real customers, shops, phone numbers or
bookings appear. Names such as "Arjun", "Joseph Antony" and the shop names are
seed fixtures.

- Location was mocked to Kanyakumari town (8.0883, 77.5385). The screens
  profile adds three fictional demo shops there (Cape Gents Hair Studio, Lotus
  Ladies Beauty Parlour, Sea Breeze Unisex Salon).
- Every shop uses the new-shop defaults: a 10% advance (₹680 → ₹68 online,
  ₹150 → ₹15) with a ₹10 minimum.
- `07-review.webp`: the coupon field's placeholder text ("e.g. FLAT100", app
  copy that names a seed coupon code) was blanked in the image. The field
  itself is unchanged.

The shop photos inside the screenshots are free stock photos, listed below.
They are placeholders until real shop photos replace them.
