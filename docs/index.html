FAQ

Good questions

How much do I pay online?

A small booking advance: a percentage of your booking total, with a minimum and a maximum, set for each shop. You see the exact amount before you pay.

You pay the rest directly to the shop when you visit, in cash or UPI.

Does every shop ask for an advance?

Not always. Some shops may not ask for one. Then nothing is paid online and you pay everything at the shop.

Can I cancel and get my advance back?

Yes, before your booking's cancellation deadline, normally 2 hours before the appointment. The app shows it as a clock time before you pay, and the deadline shown when you book is the one that applies to that booking, even if the setting changes later.

Cancel before it and your whole advance is refunded. We don't deduct gateway charges.

After the deadline you can't cancel or change the booking, and the advance is not refunded.

Can I change the time or stylist?

Yes, free, before the deadline. Move to another time or another stylist at the same shop. Your deadline then follows the new time.

What if the shop cancels?

You get your whole advance back, whenever they cancel.

What if I don't turn up?

The shop may mark the booking as a no-show, and the advance is not refunded.

A coupon on a cancelled booking is released for you to use again. On a no-show it stays used.

What happens while I'm paying?

Your slot is held for 10 minutes so nobody else can take it. If your payment completes after the hold has run out, it is refunded in full. You don't need to ask.

When will I get my refund?

We start your refund within 3 working days. Your bank usually credits it 5–7 working days after that. Nothing after 12 working days? Write to us.

Refunds go back to the way you paid, through Razorpay. OGSTYLE never sees your card, UPI or bank details.

How do shops get paid?

The advance you pay online is OGSTYLE's commission. The shop collects the rest of the price directly from you at the shop, in cash or UPI. OGSTYLE never holds or passes on the shop's money.

How do I delete my account?

Email support@ogstyle.app with the subject “Delete my account” and the mobile number you sign in with. We may ask you to confirm it from the app or with a code sent to that number.

Within 7 working days we close your account and sign you out on every device. Past bookings and payment records are kept as financial records for a set period, then deleted. The details are in our Privacy Policy.

Is the app in Tamil?

Yes. The app works in English and Tamil, and you can switch in settings.

Is there an iOS app?

OGSTYLE is coming to Android first. An iPhone app is planned for later.

The full rules are in our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
